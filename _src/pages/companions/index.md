<h3><a href="some-prime-nonsense.html">Some Prime Nonsense</a></h3>
<p class="summary">A number generator for a Magic: The Gathering combo.</p>
<div class="details">
<p>Generates a composite number larger than 2<sup>50</sup> that dodges the usual divisibility rules, so it can't be factored by hand. The defending player picks a factor, and the page checks it and shows every factor of the number.</p>
<p>A browser version of my <a href="https://github.com/Clockknight/some-prime-nonsense">Python program</a>.</p>
