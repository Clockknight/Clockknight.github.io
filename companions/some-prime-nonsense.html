Some Prime Nonsense

This generates a composite number "N" for the combo in this decklist.

N avoids the rules of divisibility and is intended to be humanly incalculable (generated numbers are larger than 250), to make guessing a factor as difficult as possible.

Generate a number, have the defending player pick a factor of it, then check that factor. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. This is a JavaScript port of the original Python program.
